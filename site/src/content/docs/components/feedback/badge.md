sidebar:
  order: 1
---
```tsx
<Badge tone="success" variant="solid">Paid</Badge>
<Badge tone="warning" variant="outline">Due</Badge>
```

- `tone`: `'neutral' | 'info' | 'success' | 'warning' | 'danger'` (default `'neutral'`)
- `variant`: `'soft' | 'solid' | 'outline'` (default `'soft'`)
